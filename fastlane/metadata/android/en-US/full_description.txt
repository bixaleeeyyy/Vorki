Vorki is a free, open-source collection of home-screen widgets that you can customise completely. No ads, no tracking, no internet access.

<b>Widgets</b>
• <b>Clock</b> – 12 styles, digital and analog, 8 fonts, and a photo clock with the time over your own picture.
• <b>Calendar</b> – the date, the date with the day, or the whole month.
• <b>Counters</b> – count down to a date, count days since something, or see how far through the year, month or week you are.
• <b>Event list</b> – your next dates in one list.
• <b>Notes</b> – a sticky note or a to-do list you can tick off from the home screen.
• <b>Quote of the day</b> – a new short quote every day, or your own.
• <b>Photo frame</b> – one photo, or a slideshow of up to 12.
• <b>Quick Tap</b> – double-tap to lock the screen, open an app, toggle the flashlight or open search.
• <b>App row</b> – up to six app shortcuts in one row.
• <b>Fun</b> – dice, coin flip, magic 8-ball, rock-paper-scissors, random number, or pick from your own list.

Pick a picture from your gallery as the background of any widget, choose one of five app colour palettes, about twenty colours plus a custom colour picker, and eight fonts. Every widget has a live preview, and widgets you already placed can be edited from inside the app.

Parts of the code were written with AI assistance. Vorki is based on "Transparent Widget" by Vishnu (EasWareApps) and is licensed under the GNU GPL v3.
